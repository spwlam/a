## What do these roles entail?

### Takeaway
The Investment Consultant is an entry-level, client-facing branch role focused on proactive outreach to existing Schwab clients and walk-in prospects, positioned as a career track toward Financial Consultant. The Financial Consultant Partner supports a Sr. Financial Consultant in managing an affluent book of business. Both are primarily asset-gathering and relationship-management roles, not call-center or operations.

### Cited Findings
- **Investment Consultant:** Described as "the first step towards becoming a Financial Consultant (FC) at Schwab." Duties include proactive outreach to existing clients, needs-based analysis, product positioning, supporting walk-in traffic, and administrative collaboration with branch FCs. — [ZipRecruiter Grand Central listing](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/c/Charles-Schwab/Job/Investment-Consultant-New-York,-NY-(Grand-Central)/-in-New-York,NY?jid=33a11a3d02152161)
- **Investment Consultant training:** New hires complete Schwab's four-week virtual "Wealth Management University" program followed by in-branch mentorship. — [workopia.io aggregator](https://workopia.io/jobs/2b07e8548af259836a79dff4376dab31)
- **Investment Consultant client profile:** IC-level clients generally have smaller portfolios; dedicated FC-level consultants typically serve clients with $500,000+ in assets. — [Schwab branch page for Grand Central](https://www.schwab.com/app/branch-services/financial-consultant/rob-graham)
- **Financial Consultant Partner:** "In partnership with the Sr. Financial Consultant(s), the Financial Consultant Partner will support the growth and management of an affluent wealth management practice." Works in a team model with 1–3 Sr. FCs. Duties: client interfacing (phone, email, in-person), CRM updates, client appointment preparation. — [ZipRecruiter FCP Del Mar listing](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/c/Charles-Schwab/Job/Financial-Consultant-Partner-Del-Mar,-CA/-in-San-Diego,CA?jid=6600c0ecd1e04b21); [schwabjobs.com West Hartford FCP listing](https://www.schwabjobs.com/job/west-hartford/financial-consultant-partner-west-hartford-ct/33727/98845073312)
- **VP, Financial Consultant:** Higher-level role; starts with a book of existing Schwab clients; includes training at Wealth Management University and a bootcamp. — [workopia.io](https://workopia.io/jobs/2b07e8548af259836a79dff4376dab31)
- Both the IC and FCP tracks are advisory/client-facing (not call center, not operations). — Multiple sources above.

### Inferences
- The career path is: Investment Consultant → Financial Consultant (VP) → Sr. Financial Consultant. The FCP role is a lateral entry point into FC-track for candidates already in advisory roles who want to support an existing FC before running their own book.
- A Series 7/66 holder from LPL would meet the license requirements for both IC and FCP roles without needing to license up first.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed exactly how long the typical IC-to-FC promotion timeline is.
- The specific asset thresholds or production quotas for ICs to advance to FC were not found in any posting.

## What are the specific qualifications required?

### Takeaway
Both Investment Consultant and Financial Consultant Partner roles require (or strongly prefer) an active Series 7 and Series 66 license, with Schwab willing to sponsor licensing for otherwise-qualified candidates. Prior financial services experience is preferred; a CFP is a plus but not required.

### Cited Findings
- **Series 7 license:** Required (or must be obtained within a set timeframe after hire) for Investment Consultant, Financial Consultant Partner, and VP Financial Consultant roles. Multiple Schwab postings list it as a hard requirement. — [schwabjobs.com Upper West Side VP FC](https://jobs.schwabjobs.com/job/new-york/vp-financial-consultant-new-york-ny-upper-west-side/33727/28732555536); [workopia.io](https://workopia.io/jobs/2b07e8548af259836a79dff4376dab31)
- **Series 66 (or 63/65):** Also required or to be obtained after hire. — [workopia.io](https://workopia.io/jobs/2b07e8548af259836a79dff4376dab31)
- **Life & Health Insurance license:** Required or to be obtained after hire. — Multiple postings
- **Experience:** FCP postings list "3+ years of Financial Services Industry Experience" as preferred. IC postings do not specify years but prefer client-facing or sales experience. — [schwabjobs.com Walnut Creek FCP](https://www.schwabjobs.com/job/walnut-creek/financial-consultant-partner-walnut-creek-ca/33727/95052398736)
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience. — [careerbuilder.com IC listing](https://www.careerbuilder.com/job-details/investment-consultant-novi-mi--15c4cb81-f98a-4daa-af4a-9174dc35f5ba)
- **CFP:** Preferred, not required. Schwab offers tuition reimbursement for candidates pursuing it. — [workopia.io](https://workopia.io/jobs/2b07e8548af259836a79dff4376dab31)
- **U.S. citizenship:** One aggregator listed this as a requirement, but this could not be confirmed against Schwab's official postings — treat with caution.

### Inferences
- A Series 7/66 licensed associate advisor from LPL Financial meets the core license requirements for both IC and FCP roles. Having the licenses in-hand (rather than needing sponsorship) is a meaningful differentiator vs. unlicensed candidates.
- 3+ years of LPL advisory experience positions the candidate well above the stated preference for FCP roles.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Schwab conducts a license transfer/re-registration at the time of hire or if FINRA U4 transfer is automatic.
- Could not confirm whether the IC or FCP roles require any Schwab-specific internal certifications beyond FINRA licenses.

## What is the compensation structure?

### Takeaway
Schwab's NYC Investment Consultant roles post base salaries of approximately $63,800–$103,400; Financial Consultant Partner roles post $67,000–$110,500; VP Financial Consultant roles post $65,000–$100,000. All roles include performance bonuses and equity/incentive programs on top of base. Total compensation (base + bonus) for established FCs is significantly higher per Glassdoor self-reported data.

### Cited Findings
- **Investment Consultant — Brooklyn:** Base $63,800–$103,400/year — [schwabjobs.com](https://www.schwabjobs.com/location/new-york-jobs/27326/6252001-5128638/3)
- **Investment Consultant — Grand Central:** Base $68,300–$97,900/year — [schwabjobs.com](https://www.schwabjobs.com/job/new-york/investment-consultant-new-york-ny-grand-central/33727/96927977760)
- **Investment Consultant — Wall Street:** Base $50,300–$96,000/year — [schwabjobs.com](https://www.schwabjobs.com/job/new-york/investment-consultant-new-york-ny-wall-street/33727/93381388224)
- **Financial Consultant Partner — Midtown:** Base $67,000–$110,500/year — [schwabjobs.com Midtown FCP](https://www.schwabjobs.com/job/new-york/financial-consultant-partner-new-york-ny-midtown/33727/91132434304)
- **VP, Financial Consultant — Grand Central:** Base ~$65,000–$100,000/year — [workopia.io](https://workopia.io/jobs/2b07e8548af259836a79dff4376dab31); [schwabjobs.com VP FC Grand Central](https://www.schwabjobs.com/job/new-york/vp-financial-consultant-new-york-ny-grand-central/33727/83776822000)
- **Glassdoor IC aggregate:** Median base ~$105K/year plus ~$19K median bonus (177 self-reported salaries, as of Aug 2026). — [Glassdoor Schwab IC Salaries](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Charles-Schwab-Investment-Consultant-Salaries-E144_D_KO15,36.htm)
- **Glassdoor FCP aggregate:** Median ~$126K total compensation (90 submissions). — [Glassdoor Schwab FCP Salaries](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Charles-Schwab-Financial-Consultant-Partner-Salaries-E144_D_KO15,43.htm)
- **Glassdoor NYC FC/Financial Consultant aggregate:** $128K–$215K/yr range listed for NYC FCs. — [Glassdoor Schwab NYC Salaries](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Charles-Schwab-New-York-City-Salaries-EI_IE144.0,14_IL.15,28_IM615.htm)
- **ZipRecruiter national average for FCP:** $103,877/year (May 2026). — [ZipRecruiter FCP jobs](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Schwab-Financial-Consultant-Partner)
- **Benefits:** 401(k) with company match, employee stock purchase plan (ESPP), health insurance, tuition reimbursement for CFP. — multiple Schwab postings
- **Incentive structure:** All postings include language such as "bonus and incentive opportunities" tied to performance. Schwab's compensation model for advisors is primarily salary-based (not commission-only), but includes variable pay tied to net new assets and client satisfaction metrics.

### Inferences
- The NYC market commands higher base pay than national averages (e.g., $68K–$97K for IC in Grand Central vs. $62K–$84K in Honolulu).
- The jump from IC to VP Financial Consultant results in a relatively modest base pay increase per posted ranges, but total comp grows meaningfully as the FC builds assets under management.
- Schwab's salary-based model (versus LPL's production/grid model) represents a structural compensation shift; upside may be lower initially but downside risk is also lower.

### Gaps
- Exact bonus/incentive formula (e.g., percentage of net new assets) was not found in any public posting.
- No data found on typical first-year total compensation for NYC Investment Consultants from official Schwab sources (Glassdoor figures are estimates).
- Jersey City Wealth Advisor roles (2026-127554, 2026-127555) at $120K–$174K base and deadline October 16, 2026 are Schwab Wealth Advisory positions — a higher tier than IC/FCP, but closest to an open senior client-facing role in the NJ metro as of this research date.
