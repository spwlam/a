## What qualifications are listed (are Series 7/66 licenses valued)?

### Takeaway
Most NYC RIA advisor-track roles at these firms require or prefer Series 65 (the standalone investment adviser representative license used by fee-only RIAs) rather than Series 7/66. However, Series 7 and 66 are explicitly valued at Rockefeller and are considered transferable at Mariner with the expectation that a Series 65 will also be obtained. CFP certification is widely preferred.

### Cited Findings
- **Cerity Partners Wealth Advisor:** Expects Series 65 within 3 months of hire. CFP and advanced degrees noted as preferred. — [The Ladders](https://www.theladders.com/job/wealth-advisor-cerity-partners-new-york-ny_85333925)
- **Pathstone VP, Client Advisory:** Requires Series 65. — [ApplyAll](https://applyall.com/jobs/us/vice-president-client-advisory-at-pathstone-jc_87c0dca3b217f3726f6a3eb4)
- **Mariner Wealth Advisor (NYC):** Requires Series 65, CFP, or CFA; 5+ years in financial planning, accounting, or legal field. Advisor profiles of current NYC Mariner staff show a mix of Series 65 and Series 7/66 holders, suggesting the firm works with both. — [Swooped / Mariner posting](https://swooped.co/job-postings/wealth-advisor-new-york-mariner-e7a39); [iCIMS Mariner](https://careers-mariner.icims.com/jobs/3725/wealth-advisor/job)
- **Mariner Senior Wealth Advisor:** Requires Series 65, CFP or CFA, 10+ years. — [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/co/Mariner-Wealth-Advisors/Jobs)
- **Rockefeller Client Associate (Stamford, CT):** Series 7 and 66 preferred (3–5 years experience). This is the clearest direct match for a Series 7/66 holder coming from a wirehouse or IBD background like LPL. — [Resume-Library](https://www.resume-library.com/job/254276413/client-associate)
- **Hightower Senior Wealth Advisor:** Specific license requirements not confirmed from public snippets; senior role likely expects CFP or CFA. — [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Hightower-Advisors/--in-New-York)
- **TD Wealth Financial Advisor Associate (Manhattan, Bronx, Westchester):** Explicitly requires SIE + Series 7 + Series 66 (or L&H equivalent), or ability to obtain; base $66,560–$99,320. (TD is a bank, not an RIA, but included for licensing benchmark context.) — [The Muse / TD Bank](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/tdbank/financial-advisor-associate-bronxmanhattanwestchester)
- **General NYC associate advisor roles:** Series 7 holders typically meet or exceed the 7-component requirements for Series 65/66 conversion. Some firms (notably Mercer Advisors) sponsor Series 65 conversions for hires coming from broker-dealer backgrounds. — [ZipRecruiter salary page](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Series-7-66)

### Inferences
- Series 7/66 are highly valued by firms with brokerage-adjacent structures (Rockefeller, former wirehouse affiliates). Fee-only RIAs like Cerity and Pathstone weight the Series 65 more heavily but often accept 7/66 as a bridge credential.
- A candidate holding Series 7 and Series 66 can obtain Series 65 registration relatively straightforwardly (Series 66 is a combined 63+65 exam; having it means one already holds the equivalent).
- The LPL background (hybrid RIA/IBD) and existing Series 7/66 licenses position the candidate well to apply to both pure RIAs and hybrid-RIA roles; framing as already meeting the fiduciary licensing requirement (via 66) should be emphasized.

### Gaps
- None of the public postings explicitly state "Series 7 required" at the target RIAs. Whether firms that list "Series 65 required" will credit a Series 66 holder as already compliant was not confirmed.

- **Mariner Senior Wealth Advisor (NYC):** $150,000–$350,000. — [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/co/Mariner-Wealth-Advisors/Jobs)
- **Hightower Senior Wealth Advisor (NYC):** $125,000–$175,000 (ZipRecruiter estimate; not employer-provided). — [ZipRecruiter](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Hightower-Advisors/--in-New-York)
- **Rockefeller Investment Associate (NYC):** $70,000–$95,000 plus variable compensation. — [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/p/68f0f1f4-b578-4df5-9663-e7fadac91a34/Investment-Associate)
- **General NYC associate advisor base:** Median $95,000 base; commission/bonus adds significantly. Glassdoor lists Mariner Wealth Advisors average total compensation at $83K–$140K for wealth advisors. — [NYC Pay Calculator](https://nycpaycheckcalculator.com/salaries/financial-advisor-salary-nyc/); [ZipRecruiter Mariner WA](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Mariner-Wealth-Advisors-Wealth-Advisor)
- **Mariner compensation structure note:** Advisors who are also licensed insurance agents receive separate commission-based compensation on insurance products sold, on top of the base salary. ### Inferences
- The most competitive total compensation for an associate advisor with 5+ years' experience and Series 7/66 appears to be in the $125K–$175K base range at Cerity or Mariner, with bonuses likely adding 15–30%.
- Equity participation at private RIAs is typically not disclosed publicly; candidates should ask in interviews about revenue sharing, phantom equity, or partnership track timelines.
- Pathstone's $175K+ VP-level role suggests a higher experience expectation (the cohort training starting in summer 2027 implies a longer onboarding runway).

### Gaps
- Bonus/incentive plan structures (% of revenue, AUM-based, discretionary) are not publicly disclosed at any of these firms.
- Equity or partnership track timelines could not be determined from public sources.

- **Mercer Advisors (ranked #1 RIA nationally by Barron's for two consecutive years per one job listing):** NYC and upstate NY postings confirmed: Senior Wealth Advisor UHNW (NYC, Aug 2026), Wealth Advisor (NYC), Financial Planner (NYC hybrid, $76,500–$90,000). Glassdoor shows 73 open positions nationally. — [ZipRecruiter Mercer](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/co/Mercer-Advisors/Jobs/--in-New-York); [Indeed Mercer](https://www.indeed.com/q-mercer-global-advisors-l-new-york,-ny-jobs.html)
- **Silvercrest Asset Management:** NYC-headquartered, $28.9B AUM, UHNW/family office focus. No confirmed open advisor postings found in this search, but the firm is a well-regarded independent RIA. — [Markletic / Top NYC RIAs 2025](https://markletic.com/blog/top-ria-firms-in-nyc-in-2025/)
- **Focus Financial Partners:** NYC-based, $400B in AUM across 65+ independent partner RIAs. Hiring would be at the individual partner firm level, not at the Focus parent level. — [Dakota 2026 guide](https://www.dakota.com/resources/blog/top-rias-in-new-york-city)
- **Corient (formerly with Summit Trail):** $21B+ added with Summit Trail acquisition (Aug 2026). ### Inferences
- Rockefeller Capital Management is the strongest outside-list match given direct Series 7/66 preference, confirmed NYC postings with recent dates, and HNWI client focus that resembles an RIA advisory track.
- Mercer Advisors is a volume-hiring RIA nationally; NYC presence is confirmed, and a Series 7/66 holder transitioning from LPL would be competitive for the Wealth Advisor or Senior Wealth Advisor roles.
- Silvercrest and Focus partner firms require direct outreach to confirm openings, as they do not appear on major job boards regularly.

### Gaps
- Mercer Advisors' specific NYC posting IDs, exact dates, and compensation details for the Wealth Advisor (not Senior) role were not confirmed beyond the aggregator data.
- Corient's post-acquisition hiring plans for the Summit Trail New York office were not confirmed.
- Bleakley Financial Group (NJ-based; Jersey City/Parsippany offices) mentioned in growth context but no specific advisor opening confirmed. Website not confirmed accessible.
