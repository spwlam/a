## What do these roles entail?

### Takeaway
Financial Consultant and Investment Consultant roles at Fidelity Investor Centers are client-facing advisory positions serving existing Fidelity customers — warm leads, not cold prospecting — with a focus on financial planning, investment guidance, and referring complex cases to wealth management partners.

### Cited Findings
- Roles are based in Fidelity Investor Centers (branch offices), working in-person with clients. — [Brokerhunter (Investment Consultant – Upper West Side)](https://www.brokerhunter.com/jobs/553144088-investment-consultant-upper-west-side-ny-at-fidelity-investments)
- The role involves serving an existing client base and warm leads — not cold prospecting or outbound sourcing. — [Fidelity posting via The Muse](https://www.themuse.com/jobs/fidelityinvestments/investment-consultant-seal-beach-ca-63fdce)
- Duties include building customized financial plans, using Fidelity's planning and guidance tools, delivering investment solutions and retirement planning guidance. — [Fidelity via Built In (Schaumburg, IL)](https://builtin.com/job/financial-consultant-schaumburg-il/6858411)
- Consultants refer clients with more complex needs to Fidelity's wealth management partners (an internal escalation path, not outsourcing). — [Brokerhunter (Investment Consultant – Upper West Side)](https://www.brokerhunter.com/jobs/553144088-investment-consultant-upper-west-side-ny-at-fidelity-investments)
- Fidelity provides an open-architecture product platform and research resources to support client service. — [Search result summary from Fidelity postings]
- Work is onsite at the assigned branch, with Fidelity shifting toward full-time onsite (~75–100% of days). — [Multiple job postings]
- Career path: The Investment Consultant role is described as a stepping stone; the path at Fidelity goes Investment Consultant → Financial Consultant → (Relationship Manager/Wealth Management). — [Fidelity careers path overview](https://jobs.fidelity.com/en/teams/financial-planning/financial-planning-roles/)

### Inferences
- Both the "Investment Consultant" and "Financial Consultant" titles cover advisor-track, client-facing work in Fidelity branches. The Financial Consultant title appears to be the more senior designation, likely requiring a book of business or demonstrated performance in the IC role.
- The asset-gathering component exists (growing client relationships, expanding AUM with existing clients) but the postings do not describe outbound asset gathering targets or quotas.

### Gaps
- No posting confirmed explicit AUM targets, sales quotas, or performance metrics.
- No official Fidelity documentation confirmed the internal progression criteria from Investment Consultant to Financial Consultant.

## What qualifications are listed?

### Takeaway
A Series 7 license plus Series 66 (or 65) are the core required licenses. Prior advisory experience and relationship-building skills are expected; CFP is preferred but not required.

### Cited Findings
- **Required licenses:** Series 07 (FINRA) and Series 66 – FINRA, or the ability to obtain Series 66 upon hire. Some postings list Series 7 and 63 plus 65 or 66. — [Multiple Fidelity NYC postings]
- Series 63 listed in some Upper West Side postings; Series 65 or 66 listed as alternatives; state registrations also required. — [Brokerhunter (Financial Consultant – Upper West Side, Oct 2, 2026)](https://www.brokerhunter.com/jobs/615122376-financial-consultant-upper-west-side-ny-at-fidelity-investments)
- **Experience:** Emphasis on relationship-building, presenting complex financial solutions, and strong communication skills. Postings ask for a track record in needs-based recommendations. — [Multiple postings]
- **CFP / Degree:** A CFP credential or finance-related degree is preferred. Fidelity offers tuition reimbursement to help earn CFP. — [Fidelity career description, multiple sources]
- **Immigration:** Fidelity will not provide immigration sponsorship for these roles. — [Multiple postings]
- Candidates must meet FINRA regulatory requirements, including background check requirements related to criminal history. — [Fidelity posting via Simplify.jobs summary]

### Inferences
- A Series 7 + Series 66 licensed candidate from LPL Financial directly meets the core license requirements without needing to acquire new licenses before starting.
- The Series 63 (listed in some postings) is separate from Series 66; however, Series 66 encompasses much of Series 63's content, so this likely refers to state-specific registrations that Fidelity facilitates.

### Gaps
- Whether specific years of experience are required (some aggregators note "mid-level" or "entry level" depending on IC vs FC title, but the original postings' exact language was not confirmed).

- **Financial Consultant – Upper West Side, NY base salary:** $60,000–$75,000 per year (Oct 2026 posting). — [Brokerhunter](https://www.brokerhunter.com/jobs/615122376-financial-consultant-upper-west-side-ny-at-fidelity-investments)

Another secondary source cites Glassdoor at $120,000–$185,000. These are self-reported aggregated figures, not official Fidelity data. — [Search result from ratings.koha-community.org (low-quality source, figures from Glassdoor)]
- **Fidelity's official representative compensation disclosure** was updated January 2026 and describes a base-plus-variable structure. The full document was not accessible. — [fidelity.com representative-compensation.pdf (inaccessible)]

### Inferences
- The base salary range ($60K–$75K) is set below market for licensed financial advisors in NYC, but variable/bonus pay appears to substantially increase total compensation to $120K–$200K+.
- Fidelity's model for Financial Consultants is salary + bonus/variable, not a pure commission structure (unlike a wirehouse model). This is a notable difference from LPL Financial's advisor compensation model.
- NYC cost-of-living adjustment may position these roles at the higher end of the base range compared to suburban NY postings.

### Gaps
- The exact bonus structure, performance metrics that trigger variable pay, and the variable pay ceiling were not found in accessible sources.
- No confirmed NYC-specific breakdown of total compensation versus national averages.
- The full Fidelity representative compensation PDF (updated Jan 2026) was not accessible for review.
