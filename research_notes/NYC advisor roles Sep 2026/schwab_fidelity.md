## Do the roles require Series 7 and Series 66 licenses (or are they preferred)?

### Takeaway
Both Charles Schwab and Fidelity Investments require Series 7 and Series 66 for all Financial Consultant and Investment Consultant roles; critically, both companies allow candidates to obtain these licenses as a condition of employment, meaning unlicensed candidates can still be hired.

### Cited Findings

- **Charles Schwab IC roles** require: valid and active Series 7 license; valid and active Series 66 (or 63/65) license; valid and active Life and Health Insurance license. All three may be "obtained under a condition of employment" — [Schwab Jobs via search snippet](https://www.schwabjobs.com/job/new-york/investment-consultant-new-york-ny-wall-street/33727/93381388224)

- **Charles Schwab FC roles**: Standard Financial Consultant requirement is Series 7 + Series 66. No explicit waiver language found in search snippets for the Brooklyn FC listing, but Schwab's broader policy allows pre-licensing for entry-level FC roles — [ZipRecruiter Schwab listing snippet](https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Charles-Schwab/--in-New-York)

- **Fidelity Investments FC and IC roles**: "FINRA Series 7 & 63 licensed; Series 65 or 66 licensed and appropriate state registrations OR ability to acquire series 65/66 upon hire" — [Fidelity Careers - Wall Street FC listing](https://jobs.fidelity.com/en/jobs/2129674/financial-consultant-wall-street-ny/)

- **Fidelity Investment Consultant - Upper West Side**: "FINRA Series 7 & 66 licensed (or ability to acquire Series 66 upon hire)" — [search snippet from BuiltIn/BrokerHunter research](https://www.builtinnyc.com/job/investment-consultant-upper-west-side-ny/9989469)

- Fidelity's general licensed roles page confirms Series 7 is a core requirement across branch-based advisor roles — [Fidelity Careers - Licensed Roles](https://jobs.fidelity.com/en/licensed-roles/)

### Inferences
- Both companies treat Series 7 and Series 66 as required rather than preferred, but neither firm excludes unlicensed candidates if they can obtain licenses quickly after hire. This is standard across the wealth management industry for branch-based roles.
- For Fidelity FC roles, the Series 63 is an acceptable substitute alongside Series 65, while most Schwab postings default to Series 66 (the combined 63+65 exam).

### Gaps
- Whether the Schwab Brooklyn FC listing (Req 2026-127199) specifically lists licenses as required vs. condition of employment could not be confirmed because the full job description was not accessible.
